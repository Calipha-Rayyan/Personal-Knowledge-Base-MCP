Personal Notes About My Interests

I love watching the sunset at the beach, especially in the evening when the sky turns orange and pink. The sound of ocean waves helps me relax.

My favorite color is blue because it reminds me of the ocean and the clear sky. I often choose blue for clothes, notebooks, and computer themes.

I enjoy eating pizza on Friday nights. My favorite pizza toppings are chicken, mushrooms, cheese, and olives.

Coding in Python makes me feel like a wizard. I use Python to build small applications, practice machine learning, automate repetitive tasks, and experiment with artificial intelligence.

My cat sleeps on my keyboard when I am working. Sometimes it sits beside my laptop and watches the screen while I write code.

I want to travel to Japan to see the cherry blossoms. I would especially like to visit Tokyo and Kyoto during cherry blossom season.

The best fruit in the world is mango. I enjoy eating fresh mangoes during summer because they are sweet, juicy, and refreshing.

I exercise every morning to stay healthy. My routine usually includes walking, stretching, and light strength exercises.

I am interested in artificial intelligence and machine learning. I want to learn more about semantic search, embeddings, vector databases, and retrieval systems.

I am building a personal knowledge base that can store documents and find relevant information using semantic search.

Semantic search means finding information based on meaning and context rather than only matching exact keywords.

Embeddings represent text as numerical vectors. Similar meanings tend to produce vectors that are close to each other in vector space.

Qdrant is a vector database that can store embeddings and perform similarity searches. It can return the most relevant chunks of information for a user's query.
